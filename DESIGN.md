## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of hyper-modern computational artistry and classical luxury craftsmanship. It serves high-growth brands, creative visionaries, and enterprise innovators seeking bespoke digital artifacts, immersive interactive environments, and high-fidelity product design.

The visual direction rejects generic SaaS uniformity in favor of a cinematic, gallery-grade atmosphere:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep-space dark canvases layered with subtle refractive luminescence and crisp spatial boundaries.
- **Controlled Duality:** The tension between dynamic technological energy (electric blues, vivid violets) and the timeless prestige of burnished metallics (warm gilded accents).
- **Restrained Precision:** Generous typographic staging, razor-thin luminous rules, high editorial standards, and deliberate micro-interactions that communicate uncompromising polish.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an obsidian architectural foundation punctuated by hyper-saturated luminescent highlights and warm, burnished gold accents.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Background (`#07090F`):** Deep abyssal tint serving as the primary viewport and base field.
- **Surface (`#0D111C`):** Foundational structural container for low-elevation surfaces, cards, and structural layouts.
- **Elevated Surface (`#111827`):** Elevated modals, interactive cards, hovering panels, and dropdown menus.
- **Border / Structural Inset (`#20283A`):** Low-contrast boundary lines providing structural integrity without optical clutter.

### Chromatic Accents
- **Primary Energy ("Twink"):** Electric Blue (`#168BFF`) provides immediate call-to-action focus. Royal Blue (`#3155FF`) acts as its supportive depth layer. Luminescent Violet (`#6C35FF`) supplies gradient shifts and chromatic ambiance.
- **Artisanal Luxury ("Myst"):** Primary Gold (`#D4A72C`) delivers signature metallic accents, editorial glyphs, and high-status badges. Light Gold (`#F3D477`) highlights specular reflection points. Deep Gold (`#A87512`) provides warm hairline borders and active ring focus.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Pure optical clarity for dominant headlines, active values, and primary body copy.
- **Text Secondary (`#A7B0C0`):** Balanced, desaturated tone for metadata, supporting editorial text, labels, and muted controls.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric posture of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and status markers with the structural legibility of Inter for continuous narrative reading.

- **Editorial Stance:** Headings utilize tighter tracking to command architectural presence, maintaining an authoritative voice.
- **Editorial Sub-elements & Eyebrows:** The `caption-caps` style must be set in all uppercase with extended letter spacing (`0.12em`), frequently rendered in `#D4A72C` (Primary Gold) or `#A7B0C0` to introduce sections with restrained curation.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body tiers retain generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.65`) against the dark background, preventing eye fatigue and sustaining luxury pacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to maximize negative space, framing components like gallery artifacts.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12 columns, `4rem` outer canvas margins, `2rem` gutters. Max content bounding box: `1400px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** 8 columns, `2rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
Layout rhythms favor asymmetry and substantial breathing room over dense packing. Vertical distances between distinct editorial blocks scale from ` space-xl` (40px) to larger multi-step rhythms (80px to 120px) to emphasize bespoke product craftsmanship.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material separation rather than stark drop-shadows.

1. **Substrate Base (Z: 0):** Pure `#07090F` background, occasionally textured with ultra-soft radial illumination (`rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.04)` or `rgba(108, 53, 255, 0.03)`) at 120px blur to suggest cinematic studio lighting.
2. **Glass & Inset Plates (Z: 1):** `#0D111C` with a structural outline of `1px solid #20283A`. Backdrop blur is set to `16px` for transparent navigation headers and floating panels.
3. **Elevated Dynamic Modules (Z: 2):** Elevated cards (`#111827`) feature subtle directional lighting from top to bottom. Outlines use a linear gradient: `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.12)` at top-left fading to `rgba(32, 40, 58, 0.4)` at bottom-right.
4. **Interactive Aura Glows:** Interactive components emit soft, localized outer glows rather than dark drop shadows:
   - Primary Interactive: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.35)`
   - Gold Accent Hover: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(212, 167, 44, 0.25)`

## Shapes

The geometric architecture follows level `2` (Rounded). This keeps corners crisp enough to feel modern and calculated, while soft enough to avoid harsh brutalist edges.

- Base inputs, buttons, and badges utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Structural containers, showcase cards, and viewports utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Modals, spotlight highlights, and dialogue structures utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Pill-shaped geometries (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for contextual status chips and floating tag labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Studio Action:** Background gradient combining Electric Blue (`#168BFF`) and Royal Blue (`#3155FF`). Text in `#F8FAFC`, semi-bold Plus Jakarta Sans. Border: 1px translucent highlight `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`. Hover triggers outer bloom: `0 0 20px rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.45)`.
- **Myst Luxury Action (Signature):** Solid `#D4A72C` or radial gold gradient with text in `#07090F` (abyssal black). On hover, brightness escalates to Light Gold (`#F3D477`) accompanied by a subtle `rgba(212, 167, 44, 0.3)` ambient glow.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Background `#0D111C` with border `1px solid #20283A`. Text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, the border transitions to `#168BFF` with an inner glow.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Status (Digital):** Background `rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.25)`, text `#168BFF`.
- **Curated (Luxury):** Background `rgba(212, 167, 44, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 167, 44, 0.3)`, text `#F3D477`.

### Cards & Artifact Showcases
- Surface: `#0D111C` base, stepping to `#111827` on elevation.
- Perimeter: 1px micro-border using `#20283A`.
- Hover state: Border shifts to top-lit gradient (`rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.5)` to `#20283A`), card transforms with an upward delta of `-2px`.

### Input Fields
- Container: Surface `#0D111C`, 1px border `#20283A`, `0.5rem` radius.
- Typography: `#F8FAFC`, placeholder text `#A7B0C0` at 60% opacity.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#168BFF` with a subtle focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.18)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box/Circle: Frame `#111827` with a 1px border `#20283A`.
- Active State: Background `#168BFF`, icon mark rendered in `#F8FAFC`. Optional luxury variant activates in `#D4A72C` with deep black glyph.

### Studio Artifact Gallery Rows (List Alternative)
- Minimal horizontal dividers (`1px solid #20283A`) dividing editorial items without heavy list styles.
- Hovering reveals a localized radial backlight along the cursor axis, illuminating the entry with a muted electric violet sheen.
